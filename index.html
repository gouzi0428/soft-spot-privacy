Privacy Policy

Company name: Soft Spot Club · Effective October 12, 2026

Company and publisher

This Privacy Policy belongs to Soft Spot Club. The company name associated with this policy and provided in the Pinterest developer application is Soft Spot Club.

This policy explains how Soft Spot Club handles information when using its private social publishing tool to prepare and publish content to its own Instagram and Pinterest accounts. The tool is operated for the account owner's use; it is not offered to the public as a consumer service.

Information the tool handles

The tool handles the social account authorization tokens needed to publish, along with the account owner's post materials, such as images, captions, keywords, selected Pinterest board, and planned publishing time. It does not ask social media viewers to submit personal information and is not designed to collect information about them.

How information is used

This information is used only to prepare, schedule, and publish content to the account owner's connected social accounts, and to report whether a publishing attempt succeeded. It is not sold, rented, used for advertising, or used to build profiles of social media viewers.

Storage and service providers

Publishing materials and scheduling settings are stored in the account owner's private GitHub repository. Authorization tokens are stored as encrypted GitHub Actions secrets and are not included in the public policy website or post content. GitHub Actions runs the scheduled publishing workflow. Instagram/Meta and Pinterest receive the content and account authorization needed to publish it. Publicly accessible image hosting may be used so the social platforms can retrieve images for publication.

These providers handle information under their own privacy policies and terms. Workflow logs may contain operational details such as run status and error messages; access to the private repository is limited by its GitHub permissions.

Sharing

Information is shared only with GitHub and the connected social platforms or image host as needed to operate the publishing workflow. Content that is published becomes visible according to the settings of the relevant social account and platform.

Retention and control

The account owner can remove queued post materials from the private repository, delete stored authorization tokens, and revoke the tool's access from the relevant platform settings. GitHub may retain workflow logs according to its own retention settings. Removing a post from the publishing queue does not remove content already published on Instagram or Pinterest; that content must be removed from the relevant platform.

Children's privacy

This publishing tool is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated if the publishing workflow or the information it handles changes. The effective date above will be revised when an update is made.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact Soft Spot Club through its official Pinterest profile.
